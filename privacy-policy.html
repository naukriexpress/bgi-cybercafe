Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026. This page explains how BGI Cybercafe handles information related to this website and assistance requests.

Information and purpose

The public website displays service information stored with Supabase. If you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, your message and details you provide are handled through WhatsApp. For an application, we may ask for the information and documents required for that specific service. We use those details to respond to you and help with the requested process.

Sharing and providers

Applications may require submission of your details to the relevant official portal or service provider. WhatsApp, Supabase, Vercel and an embedded YouTube video, when you open it, operate under their own privacy policies. Avoid sharing passwords or OTPs with us.

Retention and requests

Information needed for an assistance request may be retained as necessary to complete the work, respond to questions, maintain records or comply with applicable obligations. To ask about your information or request correction or deletion where applicable, use the contact method on our Contact Us page. We will review your request in light of the service and applicable requirements.

Changes

We may update this policy if our services or data practices change. The date above shows the latest revision.
